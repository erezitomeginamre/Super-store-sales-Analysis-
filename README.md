# Superstore Sales Dashboard - Power BI

> Business Intelligence Overview Report | Executive Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Overview
A complete Power BI dashboard analyzing Superstore performance from 2014-2017. Built for Business Intelligence assignment to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

**Live KPIs from dashboard:**
- **Total Sales:** $2.33M
- **Total Profit:** $292K
- **Profit Margin:** 13% (0.13)
- **Total Orders:** 5K
- **Average Sales:** $228.23

## 🎯 Dashboard 

** Executive Overview:**
- 5 Purple KPI Cards (Sales, Profit, Margin, Orders, Avg Sales)
- Total Sales by Region (Bar Chart) - West Dominance
- Total Profit by Category (Bar) - Technology Leads
- Total Sales by Segment (Column) - Consumer ~50%
- Sales Trend Over Time (Line Chart) - Q4 Spikes
- Sales by State (Treemap) - California Largest

**Detailed Analysis:**
- Donut Chart - Sales by Region %
- Profit Matrix Table - Category / Sub-Category breakdown
- Total Profit by Sub-Category - Copiers & Phones top

## 💡 Key Insights (Part 4)

**5 Insights:**
1. West region drives >31% of $2.33M sales
2. Technology is most profitable (Copiers & Phones)
3. Consumer segment is largest customer base
4. Low 13% margin = $13 profit per $100 sale
5. Growing but volatile with Q4 seasonality

**3 Risks:**
- Furniture (Tables/Bookcases) losses dragging profit
- Geographic concentration in California
- Low order value ($228.23 avg) = high shipping cost

**3 Opportunities:**
- Push Technology category
- Fix Central region using West playbook
- Grow Corporate segment for higher AOV

## 🚀 Recommendations (Part 5)

1. **Reallocate 30% marketing to Technology** - Q4 promo bundles
2. **Fix Furniture Pricing** - +12% price or renegotiate suppliers
3. **Replicate West Success in Central** - Training & product mix
4. **Corporate Loyalty Program** - Lift AOV from $228 to $300+
5. **Diversify from California** - Target TX, PA, NY campaigns

**Target:** Sales $2.7M (+15%), Profit $450K (+54%), Margin 16.5%
